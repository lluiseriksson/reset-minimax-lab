Research checkpoint 66 — 2026-10-24
Status: unproved conjecture; no manuscript, novelty, capacity, or grade claim.

All entropies below use natural logarithms. Let q=1-lambda, with
R_tau(rho)=lambda rho+q tau Tr rho. For pure psi on n copies, the conjecture is

 S(R_tau^tensor n(psi)) >= n[q S(tau)+h(lambda)]
                          - sum_i h(q Tr(psi_i tau)).                (C)

Exact reformulation, not a proof of (C)
------------------------------------
Introduce independent classical retention flags J_i with Pr(J_i=1)=lambda.
For retained set T, the conditional channel output is psi_T on T tensored
with tau on each of the other n-|T| systems. Thus its entropy is
S(psi_T)+(n-|T|)S(tau). The classical-quantum joint entropy identity gives

 S(B^n)=n h(lambda)+n q S(tau)+E_T S(psi_T)-H(J^n|B^n).             (1)

Here H(J^n|B^n) is the conditional entropy of the cq flag/output state;
the unflagged output alone does not supply the flags to a decoder.
Consequently (C) is equivalent, for these inputs, to

 H(J^n|B^n) <= E_T S(psi_T)+sum_i h(q Tr(psi_i tau)).              (2)

Equation (2) isolates the missing step: branch ambiguity has to be controlled
jointly, allowing entanglement entropy of retained subsets. Neither ordinary
subadditivity of output entropy nor a reverse data processing inequality
establishes it.

Established restricted case
--------------------------
For n=1 and pure input phi, measure the output with {phi,I-phi}; guess
retention on the first outcome and replacement on the second. The binary
decision error has probability e=q Tr(phi tau). Data processing followed
by the binary Fano inequality gives H(J|B)<=H(J|Y)<=h(e). Substitution
into (1), with S(phi)=0, proves (C) for one use. For product pure inputs,
output entropy and every term of (C) add, so this also proves that restricted
case for any n. These are applications of known facts, not a new theorem.

For fixed n, if (C) were proved for all pure inputs, it would extend to all
mixed inputs by concavity of S(channel(rho)) and convexity of the proposed
right side as a function of rho. This does not prove the pure entangled case.

Rejected local induction
------------------------
The stronger proposed conditional step

 S(R_tau(rho_AR)|R) >= lambda S(A|R)+q S(tau)+h(lambda)
                      -h(q Tr(rho_A tau))

is false. Already for trivial R, rho=tau=I_2/2 and lambda=q=1/2, its left
side is ln 2 while its right side is ln 2+h(1/2)-h(1/4)>ln 2.
The strict excess is about 0.130812035941137 nats. This invalidates this
proof route; it is not a counterexample to (C), which lacks lambda S(rho).

Numerical evidence, limited scope
---------------------------------
entropy-falsifier66.py independently constructs the tensor channel by a sum
over retention subsets and compares it with sequential local replacements.
It tests 3627 structured cases (Schmidt/GHZ, W, chain-graph inputs; n<=4,
d<=3 in the explicitly listed configurations), including rank-one and
nonuniform resets and lambda values .001 through .999. Minimum margin:
-4.440892098500627e-16, consistent with roundoff at equality.

48 bounded L-BFGS-B searches (24 for n=2,d=2; 12 for n=2,d=3;
12 for n=3,d=2) all returned convergence, with minimum margin
2.3582167779908845e-11. These are local searches, not global certificates.
The code retains final vectors, spectra, convergence diagnostics and all
structured cases. Thirty additional random mixed-state channel crosschecks
passed. No margin below the declared -2e-10 numerical violation threshold
was found. No interval or arbitrary-precision guarantee is claimed.
