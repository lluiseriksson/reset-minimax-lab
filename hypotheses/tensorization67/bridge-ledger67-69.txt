Larger-block falsifier
ghz-falsifier68.py uses the exact diagonal-plus-rank-one structure of weighted
GHZ code words with permuted local computational bases and diagonal reset.
After at least one replacement, tracing a GHZ site removes all coherences.
The only off-diagonal contribution is the no-replacement branch. A d by d
block therefore supplies its nontrivial eigenvalues; all remaining eigenvalues
are scalar diagonal entries. Thirty-two small cases compare this reduction
against the independent full channel construction, including entropy values.
The larger campaign covers n,d=(4,3),(5,3),(6,2),(4,4),(8,2),(5,4), 480 cases
each, plus6 local parameter optimizations in each configuration.
Results:2880 cases,36 converged optimizations,no margin below -2e-10;
minimum -4.951594689828198e-14 is within roundoff tolerance.
This excludes neither arbitrary basis rotations nor arbitrary entangled inputs.
